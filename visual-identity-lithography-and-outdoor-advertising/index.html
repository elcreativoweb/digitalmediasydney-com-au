Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

What is visual identity and why is it important for my business?

Visual identity is the set of graphic elements that represent the image and personality of a brand. It is essential to stand out in the market, create coherence and generate recognition.

How can I design an effective visual identity for my company?

We have professional graphic designers who understand your brand needs and values ​​to create a coherent logo, color palette, fonts and graphic elements.

What is the typical process for developing a visual identity?

The process involves researching the brand, its audience and competitors, conceptualizing ideas, designing sketches and prototypes, and finally implementing the visual identity across different materials and platforms.

How long does it take to develop a visual identity for my company?
The time may vary depending on the complexity of the project, but it usually takes between 2 to 8 weeks from the initial phase to full implementation, generally 2 weeks on average depending on the complexity.
What is the average cost of creating a visual identity?

Costs may vary depending on the agency or freelance designer. The price can be between $50 USD to $400 USD, depending on the scope of the project if only the logo design, imagotio or the complete visual identity is required, which includes card design, portfolio, stamp design, mockups of uses, among other aspects.

Can I use my visual identity on social media and printed materials?

Yes, your visual identity can be applied across all communication platforms, including social media, print materials, website, stationery and more.

How can I ensure my visual identity is consistent with my brand?

It is essential to establish design guidelines and a visual identity manual that details how to use each graphic element in different contexts. This way, you ensure consistency across all applications.

Can I update or change my visual identity after it is implemented?

Yes, although it is recommended to keep the visual identity consistent over time, it is possible to make updates or changes if necessary to adapt to new trends or changes in the market.
